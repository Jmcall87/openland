# Hooks & retention

The first 1–3 seconds decide everything. On-screen hook text runs 3–7 words and must match what is said or shown.

Hook formulas:
- **Curiosity gap:** "Nobody tells you this about ___"
- **Bold claim:** "This ___ made me $10k in 30 days"
- **Negative:** "Stop doing ___ (do this instead)"
- **Story drop-in:** start mid-action, with no intro
- **Number:** "3 ___ that feel illegal to know"
- **Challenge / stakes** (MrBeast): "Last to leave ___ wins ___"

Retention beats:
- Pattern interrupt every 2–3 s: a zoom, cut, B-roll, SFX or caption color change.
- Open loops: "but the third one…", and pay them off later.
- Cut every breath and filler word (silence removal does most of this).
- End on a loop line that flows into the first frame, or on a question.
- Captions: the active word is highlighted. Keep lines to 1–3 words for hype and 4–5 for talk.

Never invent results or claims about the creator. Ask if unsure.
