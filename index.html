Open OpenLand

Install on your Home Screen

Easiest way, about 10 seconds. Open this page in Safari.

  1. Tap Open OpenLand above.
  2. Tap the Share button in Safari's toolbar.
    Share
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
    Add to Home Screen
  4. Tap Add. OpenLand opens full-screen from its icon, like an app.

Or download the iPhone installer

Download for iPhone
  1. Tap Download for iPhone, then Allow. If Safari saves it to Downloads instead, open the Files app › Downloads and tap OpenLand.mobileconfig.
  2. Open Settings, tap Profile Downloaded near the top.
  3. Tap Install (top right), enter your passcode, then Install again. The OpenLand icon appears on your Home Screen.

The installer only adds the OpenLand icon (a full-screen web clip). It doesn't manage your device or read your data. iOS will call it "Not Signed"; that's normal for free profiles. Remove it anytime in Settings › General › VPN & Device Management.

On iPhone: chat, free and private AI models, Studio (ffmpeg in your browser), stock media, the in-browser Linux computer, voice and camera all work. Screen sharing isn't possible on iPhone (Safari doesn't allow it). The full Debian computer and on-device image/video models need the Android app.
